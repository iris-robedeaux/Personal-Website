TSAlbumTrueRanking

R · Last.fm data

Ranks Taylor Swift albums by what you actually listen to. It takes your Last.fm scrobbles as a CSV (exported with benfoxall's lastfm-to-csv tool) and weights listens by track, so a long album doesn't win just for having more songs. Editions and deluxe versions are folded into their parent album, and unreleased, demo, and compilation entries are left out. I'm turning it into a web page where you enter a Last.fm username and get your ranking.
